Help and tester access

How testers get access

  1. Install/open the app from the testing invitation.
  2. Create or sign in to the app with the exact email address the administrator invited.
  3. Verify the email if prompted.
  4. Enter the one-time invite code sent by the administrator.

App-store tester enrollment and in-app membership are separate. Special in-app permission is required.

Common issues

If a code fails, confirm the email matches, the email is verified, and the code has not expired or already been used. Sign out and back in after verification or an access change.

For support, email loveline082022@gmail.com. Never send passwords, patient information, government IDs, or payment details.
