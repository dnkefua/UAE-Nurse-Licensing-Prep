Privacy Policy

The Centered Nurse Academy — UAE Nursing Licensing Prep

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how The Centered Nurse Academy (“we”, “us”, the “App”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile and web application for UAE nursing licensing preparation. By using the App you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address and display name, provided when you create an account or sign in.
  • Study data: your progress, milestones, practice-test scores, study sessions, forum posts and comments that you choose to create.
  • Usage data: basic technical information (device type, app version) needed to operate and improve the App.

We do not collect financial information, government IDs, or health records about patients. We do not sell your personal data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and authenticate sign-in.
  • To save and display your study progress, scores, and content across sessions and devices.
  • To provide features you request, including the AI study tutor, community forum, and 1-on-1 video consultations.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the App.

3. Third-Party Services

The App relies on the following trusted service providers, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Hosting, Cloud Functions) — stores your account and study data securely.
  • Google Gemini API — powers the AI study tutor. Your questions are sent to generate responses; do not include personal or patient-identifiable information.
  • JSearch / RapidAPI — returns public job-listing data for the Jobs section.
  • Public news feeds (WHO, BBC, and others) and YouTube — provide health-news content in the News section.
  • Jitsi Meet — provides in-app video rooms for consultations.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure and protected by security rules that restrict access to your own records. We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to safeguard your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data.

5. Your Rights & Choices

  • You may view and update your profile and study data within the App.
  • You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us (see below).
  • You may stop using the App and sign out at any time.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for nursing students and professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and study data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Upon a verified deletion request, we remove your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
